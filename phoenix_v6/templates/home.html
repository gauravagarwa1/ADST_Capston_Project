08 / A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions. Clear answers.

Does AI decide my final grade?

No. AI provides a preliminary assessment. Your teacher reviews the work, adds feedback and publishes the final grade.

Who is Project Phoenix for?

Students, teachers and parents supporting meaningful project-based learning.

Are career recommendations guaranteed?

No. Suggestions are exploratory guidance based on skills and interests.

Can I control my portfolio?

Yes. Students choose showcased projects and sharing settings.
